What we do

Two ways we work with you

Some clients need a team to build an AI product from the ground up. Others need clear advice on where AI fits before they invest. We do both, and often one leads to the other.

Build

AI software development

We engineer production AI systems end to end: data pipelines, models, APIs and the interfaces people use.

  • Custom machine learning and deep learning models
  • LLM applications, assistants and retrieval (RAG) systems
  • Computer vision for inspection, counting and tracking
  • Integration with your ERP, CRM, MES or e-commerce stack
  • Deployment, monitoring and model retraining (MLOps)
Advise

AI consulting

We help leadership and teams decide where AI creates real value, and how to get there without wasted spend.

  • AI opportunity assessment and use-case prioritisation
  • Data readiness review and architecture planning
  • Build, buy or partner recommendations for AI tools
  • Proof-of-concept design with clear success criteria
  • Responsible AI, privacy and governance guidance

How we work

From first conversation to a system in production

Every engagement follows the same four stages, so you always know what comes next and what you are paying for.

01 1–2 weeks

Discover

We learn your process, review your data and agree on one problem worth solving, with a measurable target.

02 3–6 weeks

Prototype

A working proof of concept on your real data. You see results before committing to a full build.

03 Scoped

Build & integrate

We turn the prototype into reliable software, connected to the tools and systems your team already uses.

04 Ongoing

Operate & improve

Monitoring, retraining and support, plus documentation and training so your team can own it.

About us

We make AI practical for real businesses

Amriva Technologies is an AI software development and consulting company working with clients across industries.

Many companies know AI could help them, but are unsure where to start, which tools to trust, or how to move a pilot into everyday use. That gap is where we work.

We are a team of engineers and consultants who combine software development, data science and business process knowledge. We build custom AI solutions, from document processing and forecasting to computer vision and intelligent assistants, and we advise organisations on strategy, data and governance.

We keep projects focused. Each one starts with a clear business problem and a measurable goal, and ends with software that is documented, maintainable and owned by you.

Our mission

Turn AI from an experiment into a dependable part of how work gets done.

We measure success by what changes in your operations: time saved, errors avoided, decisions made faster.

Our vision

A world where any organisation, not only the largest, can use AI with confidence.

We make advanced AI accessible to growing companies through focused projects, fair pricing and honest advice.
